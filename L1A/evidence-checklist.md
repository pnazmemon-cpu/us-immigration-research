# L-1A Evidence Checklist

This checklist provides a research framework for organizing evidence in an L-1A case.

## 1. Qualifying Organization

☐ Foreign company documents

☐ U.S. company documents

☐ Articles/incorporation or formation documents

☐ Ownership records

☐ Evidence of relationship between foreign and U.S. entities

☐ Organizational structure

## 2. Beneficiary's Foreign Employment

☐ Employment verification

☐ Employment contract

☐ Payroll records

☐ Tax records, where applicable

☐ Job description

☐ Evidence of managerial or executive responsibilities

☐ Organizational chart

## 3. Proposed U.S. Position

☐ Detailed U.S. job description

☐ Proposed organizational chart

☐ Duties and responsibilities

☐ Reporting structure

☐ Subordinate employee information

☐ Evidence supporting managerial or executive capacity

## 4. Business Operations

☐ Business plan, where applicable

☐ Financial information

☐ Contracts or business agreements

☐ Invoices or transaction records

☐ Marketing materials

☐ Website or business profile

☐ Evidence of ongoing business activity

## 5. New Office Cases

For a new-office case, research should separately consider:

☐ Physical premises

☐ Business plan

☐ Proposed organizational structure

☐ Financial projections

☐ Staffing plan

☐ Nature of proposed U.S. business

☐ Ability to commence operations

☐ Evidence that the business can support the proposed managerial or executive position

## 6. Quality-Control Questions

Before finalizing a research analysis, consider:

1. Is the relationship between the foreign and U.S. entities clearly documented?

2. Is the beneficiary's qualifying foreign employment supported by evidence?

3. Do the proposed U.S. duties demonstrate managerial or executive capacity?

4. Does the organizational structure support the proposed position?

5. Are operational duties appropriately distinguished from qualifying managerial or executive duties?

6. If this is a new-office case, is there sufficient evidence regarding premises, business operations, staffing, and financial capacity?

## Disclaimer

This checklist is an educational research tool and is not legal advice.

Requirements and evidentiary needs may vary according to the facts of an individual case. Current statutes, regulations, USCIS guidance, and applicable case decisions should be reviewed before relying on this material.
