# L-1A Eligibility Framework

## 1. Purpose

The L-1A classification allows a qualifying U.S. employer to transfer an executive or manager from a related foreign entity to work in the United States.

## 2. Key Eligibility Areas

### Qualifying Relationship

The U.S. and foreign entities must have a qualifying relationship, such as:

- Parent and subsidiary
- Branch and headquarters
- Affiliate
- Other qualifying organizational relationships

### Qualifying Employment

The beneficiary generally must have worked for the qualifying organization abroad for the required period before seeking L-1 classification.

### Executive or Managerial Capacity

The proposed U.S. position must involve qualifying executive or managerial responsibilities.

The analysis should focus on the person's actual duties, authority, organizational structure, and role within the business.

### New Office Considerations

Where the U.S. entity is a new office, additional requirements and evidentiary considerations apply.

The business plan, financial projections, organizational structure, premises, and proposed operations may be relevant to the assessment.

## 3. Evidence Framework

Research and case preparation may consider:

- Corporate formation documents
- Ownership records
- Organizational charts
- Employment records
- Payroll documentation
- Tax records
- Financial statements
- Business plans
- Job descriptions
- Evidence of business operations
- Supporting correspondence and documentation

## 4. Research Method

A professional L-1A analysis should distinguish between:

1. Legal eligibility
2. Organizational relationship
3. Beneficiary qualifications
4. Proposed position
5. Evidence supporting each requirement

## 5. Primary Sources

Research should prioritize:

- U.S. statutes
- Federal regulations
- USCIS policy guidance
- USCIS official resources
- Relevant administrative and judicial decisions
