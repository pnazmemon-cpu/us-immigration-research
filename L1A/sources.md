# L-1A Research Sources

This page records primary and authoritative sources used for research on the L-1A Intracompany Transferee classification.

## 1. USCIS Policy Manual

U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) Policy Manual, Volume 2, Part L — Intracompany Transferees.

Official source:

https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-2-part-l

## 2. Code of Federal Regulations

8 CFR § 214.2(l) — Intracompany transferees.

This regulation addresses requirements relating to L-1 classification, including qualifying organizations, employment abroad, managerial or executive capacity, new offices, and periods of stay.

Official source:

https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2

## 3. Immigration and Nationality Act

INA § 101(a)(15)(L), 8 U.S.C. § 1101(a)(15)(L).

This provision establishes the statutory basis for the L nonimmigrant classification.

Reference:

https://uscode.house.gov/

## 4. USCIS L-1A Resources

USCIS provides information concerning L-1A Intracompany Transferee Executive or Manager classification.

Official source:

https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/temporary-workers/l-1a-intracompany-transferee-executive-or-manager

Primary sources should be reviewed for current requirements before relying on information in this repository.
