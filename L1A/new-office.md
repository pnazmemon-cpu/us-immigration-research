# L-1A New Office Research

## 1. Overview

An L-1A new-office case involves a qualifying organization that has been doing business in the United States for less than one year.

The new-office framework contains additional requirements compared with an established-office L-1A petition.

## 2. Key Requirements

### Physical Premises

The petitioner must establish that sufficient physical premises have been secured for the new office.

### Beneficiary's Foreign Employment

For an L-1A new-office case, the beneficiary must generally have been employed abroad in a managerial or executive capacity for the required qualifying period.

### Proposed U.S. Position

The proposed U.S. employment must involve managerial or executive authority over the new operation.

### Ability to Support the Position

The evidence should demonstrate that the intended U.S. operation will support a managerial or executive position within one year of approval.

## 3. Business Plan Considerations

A new-office research analysis may consider:

- Nature and scope of the proposed business
- Proposed organizational structure
- Financial goals
- U.S. investment
- Staffing plan
- Hiring timeline
- Revenue projections
- Operating expenses
- Marketing strategy
- Business development strategy
- Expected growth

## 4. Evidence Framework

Potential evidence may include:

- Lease or evidence of premises
- Corporate formation documents
- Ownership documentation
- Foreign company records
- U.S. company records
- Business plan
- Financial projections
- Evidence of investment
- Organizational chart
- Proposed staffing plan
- Job descriptions
- Market research
- Contracts or letters of intent
- Evidence of planned business activity

## 5. One-Year Development Analysis

A strong research analysis should explain how the business is expected to develop during the first year.

Questions to consider:

1. What will the U.S. company do?

2. What services or products will it provide?

3. What is the expected market?

4. How much investment is required?

5. When will employees be hired?

6. What positions will be created?

7. What responsibilities will remain with the L-1A beneficiary?

8. How will the organization develop into a structure capable of supporting a managerial or executive position?

## 6. Research Considerations

The evidence should present a credible and internally consistent picture of the proposed business.

Business plans and projections should be supported by documentary evidence where appropriate.

The analysis should distinguish between:

- Proposed business activities
- Actual business operations
- Managerial or executive duties
- Operational or administrative duties

## 7. Primary Authorities

Research should include:

- INA § 101(a)(44)
- 8 CFR § 214.2(l)
- USCIS Policy Manual, Volume 2, Part L
- Relevant USCIS Administrative Appeals Office decisions
